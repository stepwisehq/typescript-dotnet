# Project re-entry

Before acting after a fresh start, context compaction or handoff, read
[START-HERE.md](./START-HERE.md), especially its current operating procedure, and
the active section of [docs/STATE.md](./docs/STATE.md).

Current work is a comprehensive Go-faithful allocation rollout. Complete ownership
paths across owners and consumers; do not revert to one or two local changes
followed by targeted tests, benchmarks or profiling. Coordinate integration builds
and defer behavioral/performance acceptance to the broader integrated rollout.
Correctness, upstream fidelity, independent review, xUnit and actual Native AOT
acceptance remain required. Preserve required Go copies and ownership boundaries.

Use Astra coordination and GPT-6.1 Sol implementation/review delegation. Continue
autonomously across internal milestones. Keep resumable state current; do not
restart accepted work or add a process framework. Latest user directions take
precedence over historical phase instructions.
